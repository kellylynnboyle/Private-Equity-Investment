## Methodology

Ranked across the four capital-access tiers this repo tracks: RIA-distributed
SMA/fund (AG Dillon), retail interval fund/closed-end vehicle (Fundrise VCX, ARK
Venture, Destiny Tech100), secondary marketplace (Forge, EquityZen, Hiive,
Augment), and fund-of-deals/SPV access (Alumni Ventures). Institutional/direct-SPV
platforms with materially unverified fee/minimum data (North Capital PPEX, FNEX,
MicroVentures, StartEngine Secondary, Jarsy) are excluded from the ranked ten
pending better disclosure — see Confidence Flags below and their individual
`offerings/<firm>/current-offerings.md` files. All figures sourced from the
dated files already in this repo (see each row's source) plus fresh verification
run 2026-09-03; no figure below is drawn from training-data memory.

## Market/trend summary (as of 2026-09-03)

The private-equity secondaries market hit a first-half 2026 record of $118–124B in transaction
volume (Lazard Interim 2026 Secondary Market Report, published Aug 17, 2026; Evercore's parallel
review put the figure at $121B) — a 28% year-over-year increase, split roughly 51/49 between
LP-led ($63B) and GP-led ($61B) deals, with full-year volume pacing toward $250–275B on $77B of
dedicated GP-led dry powder. The same window produced a countervailing signal: PitchBook's Q3 2026
analyst note put "zombie fund" assets (PE funds 10+ years old with no clear exit path) at a record
$348.5B at end-2025, while CohnReznick counted 872 PE-backed exits in H1 2026 versus 1,210 in H1
2025 — a 28% decline even as exit values rose on concentration in larger deals. Bain data cited in
the same reporting shows global buyout technology deal value down 70% in Q1 2026, tied to
"agentic AI" resetting SaaS valuation multiples industry-wide. Against that backdrop, the IPO
backlog most relevant to this repo's tracked names remains large and AI/defense-concentrated:
SpaceX completed its IPO June 12, 2026 (Nasdaq: SPCX); Anthropic confidentially filed its S-1 on
June 1, 2026, targeting an October 2026 listing; OpenAI, Databricks, Lambda, Kraken, Cerebras, and
Discord remain in various pre-IPO stages and continue to dominate secondary-marketplace deal flow
across every Tier 1 competitor tracked in this repo (Forge, EquityZen, Hiive, Augment). One
third-party aggregator (angelinvestorsnetwork.com) cited average venture-secondary pricing around
78% of NAV versus 92–94% for top-quartile buyout pools in mid-2026 — flagged as medium-confidence,
single-aggregator-sourced, and not independently corroborated against a primary pricing report this
run.
