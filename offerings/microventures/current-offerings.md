# MicroVentures — Current Offerings

_Last updated: 2026-08-31_

## Platform status
- Full-service investment platform combining Regulation Crowdfunding, primary late-stage private-market offerings, and a secondary-liquidity matching service (MicroVentures Liquidity) that finds qualified buyers for sellers of private shares.
- All securities activity runs through **MicroVenture Marketplace, Inc.**, a registered broker-dealer and FINRA/SIPC member.

## Access & inventory
- Primary Reg CF offerings can start as low as **$100**; a distinct minimum for the secondary-liquidity product was not found in public material.
- Accreditation requirement depends on the specific offering — MicroVentures serves both broad Reg CF investors and accredited investors, spanning early-stage crowdfunding through late-stage secondary deals. This mixed retail/accredited base differs from the accredited-only gate on Forge/EquityZen/Hiive/Augment.
- No reliable current list of named late-stage holdings (e.g. specific unicorn names) was found — treat inventory as curated/deal-flow-dependent rather than a fixed catalog.

## Fees
- MicroVentures' customer-relationship disclosure states it may receive a fee for facilitating a secondary purchase or sale; the exact amount was not disclosed publicly.
