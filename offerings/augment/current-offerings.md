# Augment — Current Offerings

**Prepared for Boyle Family Office — internal research only, not investment advice.**

_Last updated: 2026-08-31_

## Platform status
- Austin-based fintech, founded 2022. Operates as a FINRA-registered broker-dealer and SEC-registered Alternative Trading System (ATS) — a licensed exchange venue, not just a matching/introduction service.
- Closed a **$12M Series A** on October 22, 2025, led by Builders VC, with Neo, Golden Section, E1 Ventures, Corp Capital, and OPCO Ventures participating. Announced alongside the close: platform trading volume had exceeded $60B cumulative.
- Reportedly at an eight-figure annual revenue run rate and profitable year-to-date (per Caplight secondary-market data); managing $400M+ in pre-IPO assets after launching a "Collective" product in summer 2025.

## Access & inventory
- Accredited investors only; verification required before trading.
- 300+ late-stage private companies browsable, including SpaceX, OpenAI, Anthropic, Stripe, Databricks.
- Ticket size: **$10K to $100M**. Minimum reportedly dropping to **$100** "soon" per the platform's own marketing — would undercut every other Tier 1 marketplace's minimum by a wide margin if it ships; unconfirmed/unverified as of this update.
- Mobile app: "Augment Private Markets" (iOS App Store).

## Fees
- Buyer/seller matched with a non-broker counterparty: **2–4%** per Augment's published fee schedule — in line with EquityZen's post-fee-cut rate, below Hiive's and Forge's upper bands.
