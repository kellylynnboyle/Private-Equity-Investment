# Alumni Ventures — Current Offerings

_Last updated: 2026-08-22_

**Firm overview:** Largest VC platform for individual accredited investors — $1.4–1.5B+ capital raised, 11,000+ investors, 1,600+ portfolio companies. Offices in Boston, NY, Chicago, Menlo Park, Manchester NH, plus new London and Tokyo offices (2026).

## Currently open offerings
Standard terms: $10K–$3M range ($10K typical minimum), 2% annual mgmt fee, 80/20 profit share, accredited investors only.

- **Alumni (school-based) Funds** — ~20 active, e.g. Green D Ventures (Dartmouth), Yard Ventures (Harvard), Spike Ventures (Stanford), Castor Ventures (MIT), Blue Ivy Ventures (Yale), Purple Arch (Northwestern), Strawberry Creek (Berkeley).
- **Focused Funds** — AI & Robotics, AI First, Blockchain/Digital Assets & Marketplaces, Deep Tech (new, July 27, 2026), Healthtech, Seed Fund, U.S. Strategic Tech, Women's Fund.
- **Diversification vehicles** — Foundation Fund, CIO Select Fund, Opportunity Fund (Diamond Club only).
- **Global Bridge Fund series** — new international line launched June 8, 2026 (UK/Japan expansion); includes Japan US Bridge Fund and a Global Alumni Syndicate (London, June 9, 2026).

## Recent launches/updates
