# North Capital PPEX — Current Offerings

_Last updated: 2026-08-31_

## Platform status
- **PPEX** is a regulated Alternative Trading System (ATS) operated by North Capital, offering secondary trading in late-stage private companies, private funds, special situations, and other exempt securities.
- Users can place standing trades or submit an RFQ (request for quote) ahead of a firm order — closer to an institutional negotiated-execution model than a live public order book.
- Securities activity runs through **North Capital Private Securities Corporation**, a registered broker-dealer and FINRA/SIPC member.

## Access & inventory
- Company-reported platform-wide activity: **1,250+ approved assets, 3,520 deals, 2.6M transactions, $8.7B total volume, 625K secondary trades**. These are company-reported figures, not independently verified, and no named individual holdings (e.g. SpaceX, Anthropic) were found in public materials.
- North Capital separately advertises accredited-investor verification, qualified-purchaser verification, background checks, and KYC/AML workflows as part of its onboarding — but a specific investment minimum and fee schedule were not found in public-facing pages.
- **Retail/RIA accessibility is unconfirmed** — the onboarding gate and whether it's practically reachable by an independent RIA (vs. institutional-only) needs direct verification before comparing head-to-head with Forge/EquityZen/Hiive.
