# Hiive — Current Offerings

_Last updated: 2026-08-22_

## Scale
- 3,000+ private companies listed for trading; $300M+ monthly transaction volume; $2B+ in live standing orders.
- Covers peer-to-peer share transfers, pooled **Hiive Funds** (Delaware SPVs via Sydecar), and an institutional-branded **HII Series** SPV line (launched mid-2025).

## Most-traded names (Q2 2026)
Top 20 most-traded names drove 86% of Hiive's Q2 2026 trading value (top 5 = over half): **Anthropic, SpaceX, OpenAI, xAI, Cerebras, Groq, Perplexity, Databricks, Stripe, Anduril**.

- Publishes the **Hiive50 Index** — equal-weighted price index of the 50 most-liquid pre-IPO securities, reconstituted quarterly by trailing-180-day liquidity score.
- Hiive listed its own shares on its own platform ("Hiive Stock," ~$54/share per one snapshot) to raise capital.

## Pricing / fees
- Live hourly-updated order book, free to view.
- Commissions (per June 2026 Form CRS): up to 4.85% buyer / 5.75% seller, tiering down at size. Standard $25K minimum (up to $100K–$250K for some high-demand direct transfers). Hiive Funds: 0% management fee / 0% carry.

## Business updates
- Nov 2025: raised a Series B directly on its own marketplace, targeting ~$650M pre-money.
- June 2026: reportedly in talks for a secondary sale valuing Hiive at ~$780M, ahead of an anticipated IPO wave.
- Founded 2021, Vancouver-based.
