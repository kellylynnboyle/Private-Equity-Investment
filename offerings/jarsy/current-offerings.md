**Prepared for Boyle Family Office — internal research only, not investment advice.**

_Last updated: 2026-08-31_

## ⚠️ Regulatory status — read before comparing to other platforms
**Jarsy, Inc. explicitly states it is NOT a registered broker-dealer or investment adviser** and does not provide investment advice. Public material reviewed did not identify a licensed broker-dealer or ATS execution partner behind its transactions. This does not by itself mean anything unlawful is occurring, but it means legal structure, the issuer/SPV mechanics, custody arrangements, transfer restrictions, and any licensed execution partner are **mandatory diligence items** before treating Jarsy as comparable to Forge/EquityZen/Hiive/Augment/PPEX — all of which run trades through a registered broker-dealer or bank-owned entity.

## Platform status
- Retail-oriented pre-IPO portal offering tokenized exposure via SPV/share structures rather than direct share ownership. Markets itself as global/available to individuals "worldwide."
- Tracxn records a **$5M seed/pre-seed round dated June 26, 2025**, led by Breyer Capital, with Karman and named angels participating.

## Access & inventory
- Marketed minimum as low as **$10** — by far the lowest ticket size of any platform in this tracker, an order of magnitude below EquityZen's $5–10K.
- Launch coverage and site pages name **SpaceX, Anthropic, Stripe**, and other companies (e.g. Gopuff) — treat these as advertised/representative availability, not confirmed continuously-investable inventory.
- Accreditation and KYC requirements were not stated consistently across the pages reviewed; access may vary by jurisdiction.

## Fees
- No public fee schedule found; site directs users to platform-specific economic terms per deal.
