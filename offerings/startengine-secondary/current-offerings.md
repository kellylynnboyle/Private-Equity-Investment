# StartEngine Secondary — Current Offerings

**Prepared for Boyle Family Office — internal research only, not investment advice.**

_Last updated: 2026-08-31_

## Platform status
- **SE Secondary** is an SEC-registered ATS, operated by **SE Primary** (not by StartEngine Crowdfunding, Inc. itself — StartEngine Crowdfunding states it is not a registered broker-dealer, investment adviser, or funding portal).
- Matches buy and sell orders **only in securities that were originally issued through the StartEngine platform** — this is the key structural difference from Forge/EquityZen/Hiive/Augment/PPEX: it's issuer-linked secondary liquidity for StartEngine's own Reg CF/Reg A+ alumni, not a general catalog of unicorn names like SpaceX or Anthropic.

## Access & inventory
- StartEngine's primary Reg CF investing is open beyond accredited investors, but eligibility for any specific secondary security is offering- and issuer-specific. No universal secondary-market minimum was found.
- No dependable current volume figure or named-company catalog (in the SpaceX/Anthropic/Stripe sense) was found in public material — inventory is inherently limited to StartEngine-financed companies, which skews toward earlier-stage names than AG Dillon's or Forge's late-stage AI/defense-heavy rosters.

## Fees
- Regulation Best Interest disclosure: commissions on SE Secondary or SE Bulletin Board sales can run **up to 5% of the price of securities sold**.
