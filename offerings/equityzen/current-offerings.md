## Minimum investment
- Single-company: $10,000 standard, select $5,000 slots.
- Multi-company/Growth Opportunity Funds: $50,000 standard, limited $20,000 slots.
- Curated thematic funds (4–25 companies): $20,000.
- Accredited investors only.
