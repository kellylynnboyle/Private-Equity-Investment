# AG Dillon & Co. — Competitive Landscape

_Research notes, compiled 2026-08-22_

## Context

AG Dillon & Co. (AGD) is a small, RIA-distributed manager (31 funds, ~$319M AUM)
built around single-company pre-IPO secondary access, offered through two
wrappers: the Bi-Annual Fund Offerings (per-fund subscription, capped at 99
investors/fund, 12 deals/year across 2 closes) and the newer Pre-IPO Stock SMA
(pooled trade rotation, no stated investor cap, 20–24 deals/year across 3
scheduled closes plus opportunistic closes, ~95% less paperwork than holding
the same positions fund-by-fund). Both wrappers route into the same
AGDF-series funds — same manager, same 3% first-year fee + 10% carry at the
fund level, same underlying risk. Custodian cost is roughly a wash between the
two ($400–600/year for a five-position portfolio); the real tradeoff is
capacity, deal flow, and admin load (SMA) vs. custodian flexibility — Schwab or
Fidelity — that Bi-Annual preserves and the SMA (locked to CNB) gives up.

There is no independent, third-party-published "AG Dillon competitor set."
The list below is inferred from business-model overlap — firms competing for
the same RIA mandate ("give my clients pre-IPO exposure") — not from a source
that names AG Dillon alongside them explicitly. Treat it as a starting point
for further diligence, not a vetted comparison.

## Tier 1 — Same mechanic: single-name secondary access

These are the closest structural competitors: they let an advisor or investor
select specific pre-IPO companies via secondary-market purchases, rather than
buying a blended basket — the same value proposition as AGD's fund/SMA access.

- **Forge Global (NYSE: FRGE)** — Largest and most direct comparison. Publicly
  traded, runs a dedicated wealth-manager/RIA channel, lets investors select
  individual companies off its marketplace.
- **EquityZen** — Over a decade serving both individual accredited investors
  and advisors; historically leans more on diversified multi-company funds but
  also offers single-company deals.
- **Hiive** — Newer entrant in the same secondary-marketplace category, now
  showing up alongside Forge and EquityZen in third-party platform comparisons.

## Tier 2 — Same asset class, diversified-basket wrapper

Lower-effort, lower-control alternatives: an advisor gets pre-IPO exposure
without picking individual names.

- **Fundrise Innovation Fund** — Interval fund, open even to non-accredited
  investors, diversified basket of late-stage private names.
- **ARK Venture Fund** — Interval fund structure, diversified.
- **Destiny Tech100 (DXYZ)** — Publicly traded closed-end vehicle holding
  late-stage private stakes; an advisor can buy it like a stock.
- **Alumni Ventures** — Markets VC fund/SPV access to accredited investors and
  RIAs through a fund-of-deals model rather than single-name picking.

## What still needs verification before citing externally

- AUM, fee structure, and RIA-channel specifics for Forge and EquityZen —
  they're the two competing head-on for the same secondary deal flow AGD
  sources from.
- Whether any of these firms' RIA programs directly overlap with AGD's
  advisor base (no confirmed source ties them together today).
