# AG Dillon & Co. — Competitive Landscape

_Prepared: 2026-08-22_

## Context

AG Dillon's positioning is narrow enough that there's no single, published "competitor list" for the firm — it's a small, RIA-distributed shop (31 funds, ~$319M AUM) built around single-company pre-IPO secondary access, wrapped in an advisor-friendly SMA/fund structure (the Pre-IPO Stock SMA and the Bi-Annual Fund Offerings both ultimately place capital into the same AGDF-series pre-IPO funds, at the same 3% first-year fee + 10% carry, under the same manager).

Based on what actually competes for the same RIA dollars and the same investor use case, the field breaks into two tiers.

## Tier 1 — Direct structural competitors

Firms offering the same core mechanic: an advisor or investor selects individual pre-IPO names via secondary-market purchases, rather than buying into a blended basket.

- **Forge Global (NYSE: FRGE)** — the closest direct comparison. Publicly traded, runs a dedicated wealth-manager/RIA channel, and lets investors select specific companies off its marketplace — the same "you pick Anduril, you pick Baseten" value proposition AG Dillon sells.
- **EquityZen** — over a decade serving both individual accredited investors and advisors. Historically leaned more on diversified multi-company funds but also offers single-company deals.
- **Hiive** — newer entrant in the same secondary-marketplace category, increasingly appearing alongside Forge and EquityZen in third-party platform comparisons.

## Tier 2 — Asset-class overlap, different wrapper

Firms competing for the same "give my clients pre-IPO exposure" mandate, but via a diversified basket rather than à la carte name selection — lower effort, lower control than AG Dillon's model.

- **Fundrise Innovation Fund** — interval fund, open even to non-accredited investors, holding a diversified basket of late-stage private names.
- **ARK Venture Fund** — same interval-fund structure, diversified.
- **Destiny Tech100 (DXYZ)** — publicly traded closed-end vehicle holding late-stage private stakes; an advisor can buy it like a stock.
- **Alumni Ventures** — markets VC fund/SPV access specifically to accredited investors and RIAs, through a fund-of-deals model rather than single-name picking.

## Caveat

There is no published "AG Dillon competitor set" from a research firm — this list is inferred from business-model overlap in the RIA / pre-IPO-access space, not from a source naming AG Dillon alongside these firms explicitly. Verify AUM, fee structure, and RIA-channel specifics directly with Forge and EquityZen before citing numbers in a pursuit brief or client conversation, since those two compete most directly for the same secondary-market deal flow AG Dillon sources from.
